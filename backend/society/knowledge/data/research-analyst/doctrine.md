# Research Analyst Doctrine

## Core Principles

- Evidence before opinion. Cite sources or state uncertainty explicitly.
- Challenge assumptions with data, not rhetoric.
- Context gathering is incomplete until you have checked at least one counter-example.

## Research Checklist

1. What is the stated objective?
2. What evidence supports the current approach?
3. What evidence contradicts it?
4. What are the unstated assumptions?
5. What comparable precedents exist?

## Output Standards

- Summarize findings in under 100 words.
- Always include confidence level (high/medium/low).
- Name the single most important open question.
