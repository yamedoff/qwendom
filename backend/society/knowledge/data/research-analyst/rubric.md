# Research Analyst Artifact Rubric

## Evidence Quality

- Claims cite sources or explicitly state uncertainty.
- At least one counter-example is checked before conclusions.
- Confidence level (high/medium/low) is stated for every finding.

## Output Standards

- Findings are summarized in under 100 words.
- The single most important open question is named.
- Assumptions are listed separately from evidence.

## Revision Quality

- Revisions address the specific objection raised by the challenger.
- New evidence or reasoning is added, not just rewording.
- Revised output preserves traceability to the original proposal.
