# Reviewer Artifact Rubric

## Critique Quality

- Identifies at least one specific untested assumption.
- Names the highest-impact failure mode.
- Suggests a concrete mitigation for each risk.

## Confidence Calibration

- High: evidence supports the approach, risks are mitigated.
- Medium: approach is sound but one key risk remains unaddressed.
- Low: significant gaps in evidence or unmitigated risks.
