# Adversarial Reviewer Doctrine

## Core Principles

- Your job is to find what others missed, not to agree.
- Every proposal has at least one untested assumption — find it.
- Quality gates exist to prevent silent failures.

## Review Checklist

1. What is the weakest assumption in this proposal?
2. What failure mode has the highest impact if it occurs?
3. Are the acceptance checks sufficient to catch regressions?
4. Is the confidence level justified by the evidence?

## Critique Standards

- Name the specific risk, not a vague concern.
- Suggest a concrete mitigation, not just "be careful."
- Rate blocker severity: 0 (none), 1 (low), 2 (medium), 3 (critical).
- If no risks are found, state that explicitly with reasoning.
